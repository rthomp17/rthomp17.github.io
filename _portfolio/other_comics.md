[Leave her, Johnny (2020)](https://thetech.com/2020/04/15/leave-her-johnny) - Drawn and published during the week students had to evacuate MIT's East Campus dorm due to the COVID-19 Pandemic. A version with restrospective commentary added in ~2022 can be found [here](https://rthomp17.github.io/_pages/leave_her_johnny_captions.pdf). 

[Rebuild (2020)](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/bostoncomics/starbound-a-space-science-fiction-comics-anthology) - A short comic published in the Starbound anthology, about a young girl who sees a mysterious light in the sky and decides to find the source.
