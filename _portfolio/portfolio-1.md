R.Bot and Crow is a series of short educational comics about robotics and embodied AI. In particular, they’re comics exploring hard problems in those fields: what makes those problems hard, how to think about them, and what tools researchers and engineers use to solve them! I post them online at https://www.rbotandcrow.com.
